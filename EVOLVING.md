[2026-06-28] @code-builder ImGui v1.91+ API Breakage for TextColored — In newer ImGui (v1.91+), `TextColored()` takes `const ImVec4&` instead of `ImU32`. Fix: either use `ImGui::ColorConvertU32ToFloat4(imcolor)` to wrap `ImU32` results, or convert `IM_COL32(R,G,B,A)` to `ImVec4(R/255.0f, G/255.0f, B/255.0f, A/255.0f)`.
